Dentistry without the dread.

Unhurried check-ups and gentle treatment at Universal Dental Clinic, with every step explained before it happens. Nervous patients very welcome.

Baclaran branch
Inside the mall

The chair is often the hardest part of seeing a dentist. So we slow down. We tell you what we’ve found, what we suggest and why, and we only begin when you’re ready.

Your visit, step by step

  1. 01

    Walk in from the mall

    Look for the blue sign. Inside is a bright, quiet lounge, away from the bustle of the corridor, where you can settle before your appointment.

  2. 02

    Talk it through first

    Your dentist examines your teeth, explains what they’ve found and walks you through the options in plain language. Nothing starts until you’re comfortable with the plan.

  3. 03

    Gentle, careful treatment

    Treatment goes at your pace, with protective gear and sterilised instruments for every patient. Raise a hand whenever you need a pause.

Treatments

From a routine clean to a wisdom tooth, the same calm approach applies. Not sure what you need? Start with a check-up.

  • Check-ups and cleaningA thorough examination and professional clean.
  • FillingsTooth-coloured repairs that blend with your smile.
  • ExtractionsIncluding wisdom teeth, with clear aftercare advice.
  • BracesStraighten crowded or crooked teeth over time.
  • WhiteningLift stains safely under a dentist’s supervision.
  • Crowns and denturesRestore missing or weakened teeth.
